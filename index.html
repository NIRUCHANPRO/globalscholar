      📋 Master Document Checklist for Foreign Scholarships

      1. Valid Passport: Sabse pehle apna Passport banwao (kam se kam 2 saal validity honi chahiye).
      2. Academic Marksheets & Transcripts: Class 10th, Class 12th (aur agar Master's ke liye apply kar rahe ho toh Bachelor's degree transcript) English mein attested.
      3. Letters of Recommendation (LOR): Apne school Principal ya 2 teachers/professors se signed recommendation letter.
      4. Statement of Purpose (SOP) / Study Plan: 500–800 words ka essay ki tum wo course aur country kyun chuna chahte ho aur tumhare projects kya hain.
      5. English / Language Proof: IELTS / TOEFL / Duolingo English Test (Ya School se "Medium of Instruction in English" certificate—kaafi scholarships isse IELTS maaf kar deti hain!).
      6. Medical Fitness Certificate: Government hospital se signed health checkup form (MEXT, Russia aur CSC China mein zaroori hota hai).
