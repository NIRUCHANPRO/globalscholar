      📋 Complete Study Abroad Document & Timeline Guide

      1. Passport (Mandatory): Apply for your passport at least 6 months before scholarship deadlines.
      2. Academic Transcripts: Class 10th & 12th Marksheets (for UG) or Bachelor's Degree Transcripts (for Master's/PhD). Get Principal/Controller attestation.
      3. Letters of Recommendation (2 LORs): Signed letters from 2 teachers/professors highlighting your rank, coding/science projects, and character.
      4. Statement of Purpose (SOP) / Research Plan: 600–1,000 word essay explaining why you chose this major, your achievements, and future goals.
      5. English Proficiency / MOI Certificate: IELTS / TOEFL / Duolingo score, OR an official "Medium of Instruction in English" letter from your school/college.
